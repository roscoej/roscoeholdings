Software and product consulting.

Roscoe Holdings Group, Inc. helps companies plan, build, and run software products. We work with operators and technical teams on product strategy, engineering, and the systems behind them.

01Services

From the first plan to the system in production.

  • Product strategy

    Discovery, roadmaps, and pricing for new and existing software products. We turn business goals into a plan a team can ship.

  • Software development

    Design and build of web applications, internal tools, and APIs, from a first version through to production.

  • Technical advisory

    Architecture reviews, technology selection, and hands-on guidance for founders and engineering leaders.

  • Systems and integrations

    Payments, billing, and operations systems, connected to the tools a business already runs on.

02Engagements

Clear scope. Clear billing.

  1. Written scope

    Every engagement starts with a written scope that sets out the work, the timeline, and the fees.

  2. Invoiced billing

    Work is billed by invoice, monthly or by milestone, as agreed in the scope. Each invoice states its due date.

  3. Secure payment

    We accept ACH bank debit, bank transfer, and card. Payments are processed through Stripe.

03Policies

Refunds, disputes, and cancellation.

Refunds

Fees cover work already performed and are not refundable once that work is delivered.

If an invoice contains an error, or you paid in advance for work that was not delivered, we refund the difference to the original payment method within 10 business days.

Disputes

If you have a question about a charge, contact us within 30 days of the invoice date. We will review it with you and respond within 5 business days.

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank so we can resolve it directly.

Cancellation

Either party may end an engagement with 30 days' written notice. Work performed through the end date is billed at the agreed rates, and prepaid fees for work not yet performed are refunded.

A standing ACH debit authorization may be revoked with 30 days' written notice.
